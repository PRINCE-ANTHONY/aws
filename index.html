04 / A few good questions

Start where
you are.

No cloud background required. Bring curiosity, follow a question, and take the next step at your pace.

What is the AWS Student Builder Group?
A student-led community focused on exploring cloud technology through learning, hands-on building, and peer sharing.
Do I need cloud experience before I start?
No. We begin with the fundamentals, ask questions as we go, and build understanding one concept and service at a time.
Will attending cost me any money on AWS?
All projects are designed to stay within AWS Free Tier limits (Lambda, S3, DynamoDB). We also supply sandbox credits for deeper builds.
What does “building together” mean?
Learn in the open: deploy code, share what worked, document the trade-offs, and make it easier for someone else to build from there.
