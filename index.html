Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Build Floor community?
An industrial student engineering community focused on building real cloud infrastructure through hands-on architectures, live build nights, peer code reviews, and production deployments.
Do I need prior cloud experience before I join?
None at all. We start from ground zero: understanding what a server is, how APIs work, and scaling step-by-step into serverless, DynamoDB tables, and generative AI with Amazon Bedrock.
Will attending cost me any money on AWS?
Zero. All our workshops are designed strictly within AWS Free Tier quotas. We also distribute sandbox credits for deeper projects and hackathon builds.
What happens during a Build Night?
We open with a 15-minute architectural schematic teardown, followed by 60 minutes of guided hands-on deployment on your laptop, and conclude with peer demos and pizza.
Can I bring my own project or startup idea?
Absolutely. Many members use build nights to architect hackathon projects, research pipelines, and campus club backends with feedback from peers.
